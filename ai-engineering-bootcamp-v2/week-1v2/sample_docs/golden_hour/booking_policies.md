# Golden Hour Coffee Co. Booking Policies (fictional sample business)

## Scheduling

Golden Hour Coffee Co. is based in Newport Beach, California, and caters events across Orange County. We are available seven days a week, subject to scheduling. Typical event service runs between 7 AM and 9 PM Pacific time. Bookings require at least 72 hours' notice.

## How booking works

Every booking request is reviewed by the owner. A booking is only confirmed after owner approval, and a 30% deposit is required to secure a confirmed booking. The remaining balance is due seven days before the event. Submitting a request does not confirm a reservation. Payments are not processed online in this demo.

## Venue requirements

The event location must have a suitable parking spot for the coffee trailer, and the venue must permit coffee trailer service. The customer is responsible for confirming venue restrictions. Suitable electrical power must be available, or alternate arrangements must be approved by the owner. Final pricing is subject to verification of applicable taxes, fees, and unusual venue requirements.

## Travel fees (fictional service zones, not real travel estimates)

- Newport Beach, Costa Mesa, Irvine, Santa Ana: $0 travel fee
- Huntington Beach, Laguna Beach, Anaheim: $50 travel fee
- Fullerton, San Clemente: $100 travel fee
- All other locations: travel fee set by manual owner review

## Cancellation

If you cancel at least 14 days before the event, the deposit may be transferred to a future booking, with owner approval. If you cancel fewer than 14 days before the event, the deposit is generally non-refundable. Exceptions are determined by the owner.
