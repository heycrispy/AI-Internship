# Golden Hour Coffee Co. Catering Packages (fictional sample business)

Golden Hour Coffee Co. is a mobile specialty coffee trailer from Newport Beach, California, offering on-site espresso catering. Every package includes two baristas, our standard espresso menu, and full setup and cleanup.

## Package A: The Sunrise ($550)

The Sunrise includes up to 50 guests and two hours of service with two baristas, the standard espresso menu, and setup and cleanup. Additional guests are $8 each. Additional hours are $150 per hour. Maximum capacity for The Sunrise is 75 guests.

## Package B: The Golden Hour ($850)

The Golden Hour includes up to 100 guests and three hours of service with two baristas, the standard espresso menu, two specialty drinks, and setup and cleanup. Additional guests are $7 each. Additional hours are $150 per hour. Maximum capacity for The Golden Hour is 150 guests.

## Package C: The Sunset ($1,300)

The Sunset includes up to 150 guests and four hours of service with two baristas, the standard espresso menu, three specialty drinks, custom drink signage, and setup and cleanup. Additional guests are $6 each. Additional hours are $175 per hour. Maximum capacity for The Sunset is 200 guests.

## Pricing rules

- Events above 200 guests require a custom quote from the owner.
- Package pricing does not decrease if you request fewer guests or service hours than the package includes.
- Extra hours are billed in full-hour increments; partial hours are rounded up.
- A package cannot be booked above its maximum guest capacity. Choose a larger package instead.
- All quotes are estimates. Applicable taxes and fees are verified by the owner before confirmation.
