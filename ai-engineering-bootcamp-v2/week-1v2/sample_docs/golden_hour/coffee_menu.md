# Golden Hour Coffee Co. Coffee Menu (fictional sample business)

## Standard espresso menu (included in every package)

Espresso, Americano, Cappuccino, Latte, Vanilla Latte, Caramel Latte, Mocha, Matcha Latte, and Hot Chocolate. Hot and iced versions are available where appropriate.

## Milk options

Whole milk, oat milk, and almond milk are available for every drink at no extra charge.

## Specialty drinks

Our specialty drinks are the Honey Lavender Latte, the Brown Sugar Shaken Espresso, and the Iced Vanilla Oat Latte. The Golden Hour package includes two specialty drinks and The Sunset package includes three. The Sunrise package includes the standard menu only.

Seasonal drinks are subject to availability.
